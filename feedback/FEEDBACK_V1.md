## Items, as a checklist

1. Better-looking champion stats (health, armor, magic resist ...) in the unit panel.
2. Smoother graphics: characters and arena look jagged / sketchy.
3. An effect when a hero levels up (star up); a 1-2-3 star look on the health bar.
4. The shop must be open in every Planning phase.
5. Traits must not disappear when units die in a fight.
6. Najmi: opens at 2; cash-outs only at 100/200/300/400/500/600; the items scale with what was banked; must not block the shop or keep asking; follow TFT's Anima (items, cash-outs, progress bar).
7. Losing costs more health as the stages go on.
8. Items at the bottom left: replace the front-left brazier corner with a flat area and put the items there.
9. Items can be placed during combat.
10. Hovering a trait shows the champions that carry it.
11. Prismatic trait tiers only with emblems.
12. PvE rounds drop items.
13. Sound effects.
14. The first PvE drops at least 3 Item Removers.
15. The other two stage-1 PvE rounds drop 3 components.
16. Unit names sit too far above the units.
17. Health and mana bars in the Planning phase too.
18. Item icons that look like items (TFT style), not small models.
19. Hotkeys: F buys XP (works), D rerolls, E sells.
20. Hexa unlocks like TFT's T-Hex: not in the shop until unlocked.
21. Damage dealt / damage taken (blocked) stats like TFT.
