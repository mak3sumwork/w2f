## Read as
1. Performance: ~24 fps. Make it faster, and add a Settings menu with Low / Medium / High quality.
2. Camera: shows too little of the far side (the enemy bench is cut off); add zoom in / out (mouse wheel).
3. Item slots: items on the item bench can be moved between slots (reordered / swapped) (assumed reading of "replaceable").
4. Equipping: dropping an item should work anywhere on the unit's body, not only over its head; drops land on the wrong unit (one below) -- fix item dragging.
5. Hover panels (traits, units, damage chart): like TFT, the detail panel opens beside the row and stays while the cursor moves into it; hovering a champion icon inside shows that champion's details. Panels must not vanish as soon as the cursor moves.
6. The damage chart: hovering a row shows the unit's name (and its numbers). Menus should look like real TFT, open smoothly, not lag.
7. The map and the units are a little dark: brighter.
8. A custom cursor like the old League of Legends cursor.
9. Planned champions' shop cards: a sign in the TOP LEFT corner.
10. Units: click once to pick up, click again to drop (as well as press-drag-release).
11. Scouting another player: your own units are hidden, like TFT.
